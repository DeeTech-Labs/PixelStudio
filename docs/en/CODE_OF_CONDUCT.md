# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

> **Русский:** [../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

**Acceptable:** empathy and kindness; respectful disagreement; constructive feedback; accountability; focus on community benefit.

**Unacceptable:** sexualized language or imagery; trolling or insults; harassment; publishing others' private information without permission; other unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers clarify standards and take fair corrective action.

## Scope

Applies in community spaces and when representing the community publicly.

## Reporting

GitHub [Issues](https://github.com/DeeTech-Labs/PixelStudio/issues) or [security advisories](https://github.com/DeeTech-Labs/PixelStudio/security/advisories/new) when appropriate.
